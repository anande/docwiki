    Technical Blueprint and Curricular Syllabus for Mastering Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift

    Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift (RHOSO) represents a fundamental shift in how enterprise Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) is designed, deployed, and managed. By migrating the traditional OpenStack control plane from legacy TripleO-directed bare-metal deployments into a podified, container-native architecture hosted on Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform (RHOCP), RHOSO delivers an efficient, declarative control plane while maintaining a highly performant, distributed data plane on bare-metal Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) nodes.
